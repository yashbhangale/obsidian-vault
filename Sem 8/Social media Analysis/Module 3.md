### **1. What is Social Media Text Analytics?**

Social Media Text Analytics is the ==_process of analyzing text data from social media platforms_== (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn) _to extract insights, trends, sentiments, and user behavior_. It helps businesses and researchers understand public opinions, customer feedback, and emerging trends.

---

### **2. Steps in Text Analytics**

1. **Data Collection** – Gather text data from social media platforms via APIs, web scraping, or data dumps.
2. **Data Preprocessing** – Clean the data by removing stopwords, punctuation, special characters, and correcting spelling errors.
3. **Tokenization** – Break the text into smaller units (words or phrases).
4. **Normalization & Stemming/Lemmatization** – Convert words into their root form (e.g., "running" → "run").
5. **Feature Extraction** – Convert text into numerical data using techniques like TF-IDF, word embeddings, or bag-of-words.
6. **Sentiment Analysis** – Identify the polarity (positive, negative, neutral) of the text.
7. **Topic Modeling** – Group similar words and phrases to identify discussion topics (e.g., LDA - Latent Dirichlet Allocation).
8. **Visualization & Interpretation** – Use charts, word clouds, and reports to present insights.
9. **Decision Making** – Use insights for marketing, brand management, customer service, etc.

---

### **3. Key Metrics to Track in Social Media Analytics**

1. **Engagement Metrics** – Likes, comments, shares, retweets.
2. **Reach & Impressions** – Number of people who see the post.
3. **Sentiment Score** – Analyzes whether the sentiment is positive, negative, or neutral.
4. **Hashtag Performance** – Tracks the reach and effectiveness of hashtags.
5. **User Growth Rate** – Percentage increase in followers over time.
6. **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** – Percentage of users who click on a link in a post.
7. **Customer Support Metrics** – Response time, resolution rate of queries.

---

### **4. Types of Social Media Text**

1. **Dynamic Text** – Continuously changing text, such as live tweets, comments, trending topics.
    - _Example:_ Twitter posts, real-time news updates.
2. **Static Text** – Fixed text that doesn’t change over time, such as social media bios, about sections, or archived posts.
    - _Example:_ Company descriptions on LinkedIn, old blog posts.

---

### **5. Purpose of Text Analysis in Social Media**

1. **Sentiment Analysis** – Determines user sentiment (positive, neutral, negative).
    - _Example:_ Analyzing customer reviews of a product to see if feedback is mostly positive.
2. **Trend Mining** – Identifies popular topics or viral content.
    - _Example:_ Detecting trending hashtags like #Oscars2025.
3. **Intention Mining** – Understands user intent (buying intent, complaint, inquiry).
    - _Example:_ Analyzing tweets like "Thinking of buying a new iPhone" to predict purchase behavior.
4. **Concept Mining** – Extracts meaningful themes from text data.
    - _Example:_ Identifying key topics in COVID-19 discussions, such as "lockdown," "vaccine," and "work-from-home."

### Social media hyperlink analytics

- sm hyperlink analytics focuses on analyzing links shared across social media to track engagement, traffic and influence 

1. Click Tracking – Measures the number of clicks on shared links.
2. Referral Traffic – Analyzes traffic driven to websites from social media.
3. Engagement Metrics – Tracks likes, shares, and comments on posts containing links.
4. Shortened URLs Analysis – Evaluates link performance using services like Bit.ly or TinyURL.
5. Influencer Identification – Identifies users who generate high traffic through shared links.
6. Network Analysis – Examines how hyperlinks spread across platforms and networks.
7. SEO Impact – Assesses backlinks from social media for search engine ranking.

### Types of Hyperlinks
- Inlink – A hyperlink that points to a webpage from another page within the same website (internal link).
- Outlink – A hyperlink that directs users from one website to another (external link).
- Colink – When two different webpages are linked by a third common webpage.
